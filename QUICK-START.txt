LANTERN DECK - WINDOWS + SAMSUNG PHONE

1. Double-click Lantern Deck.exe on your Windows PC.
   No Python installation, command prompt or paid software is needed.
   Your PC controls open automatically in your browser.

2. Click Customize deck and assign the shortcuts you configured in
   TikTok LIVE Studio. Only assigned buttons appear on your phone.

3. Connect your Samsung phone to the same private network as your PC.
   Scan the QR code in the Lantern Deck window using your phone camera.
   Open the link, then enter the pairing code shown under the QR code.
   You can also type the address shown above the QR code into your browser.

4. Try your buttons in rehearsal, which sends no keys.
   When ready, check Enable keyboard control in the PC window.
   There is no need to restart or pair again when switching modes.

Keep the Lantern Deck window open. Closing it stops phone control.
On each new launch, it starts in rehearsal and you must pair your phone again.
If more than one network address appears, select your home network adapter.
If no address appears, connect your PC to Wi-Fi/Ethernet and restart the app.

If Windows asks for network access, allow only your trusted Private network.
Do not disable your firewall or expose the app to the internet.
This local app uses HTTP and is intended for a trusted home network.

Settings are saved in %LOCALAPPDATA%\LanternDeck\deck.json.
Use Import existing deck to bring in a deck.json from the previous version.
Backups are made before replacing an existing saved configuration.

This build is unsigned. Windows may show a reputation warning.
The app does not change Windows security settings or require administrator access.

The phone reports shortcut delivery, not TikTok's actual state.
Test your LIVE Studio shortcuts while offline before using them on a broadcast.
Physical Samsung and actual TikTok shortcut behavior still need verification.

To remove: close the app and delete Lantern Deck.exe. Your saved deck stays
in the data folder above unless you remove it separately.

UPDATE 1.1 - EASIER SHORTCUTS
Close the old Lantern Deck window, extract the new ZIP into a folder,
and double-click the new Lantern Deck.exe. Your saved buttons stay in
%LOCALAPPDATA%\LanternDeck\deck.json. Pair your phone with the new code.

In Customize deck:
- Click Add all four starter buttons, or choose a single ready-made button.
  Existing shortcuts are preserved. Assign the displayed shortcuts to the
  matching actions in TikTok LIVE Studio, then Save changes.
- Click Record shortcut, press and release the keys on your PC, then Save.
  Escape cancels; recording ends after 15 seconds or if the window loses focus.
- Choose keys instead lets you select Left Alt, Right Alt, Ctrl, Shift and
  a key without pressing them. Choose Left Alt and leave Key as None for
  Left Alt alone. Click Use these keys, then Save changes.
- Browser-reserved combinations should be selected with the dropdowns.
  Recording physical keys can also activate shortcuts in other running apps.

Starter suggestions: Microphone Ctrl+Shift+M; Main scene Ctrl+Shift+1;
Close-up Ctrl+Shift+2; Be right back Ctrl+Shift+3.
These are suggestions, not built-in TikTok defaults or automatic integration.
