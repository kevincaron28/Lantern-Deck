LANTERN DECK 2.0 + MAISON PANEL

Use the Deck tab on your home panel. No Elgato app or keyboard shortcuts
are needed in Maison mode.

ON YOUR WIFE'S PC (or Kevin's PC for testing):
1. Close the old Lantern Deck. Extract this ZIP and run Lantern Deck.exe.
2. Open TikTok LIVE Studio to its main studio screen.
3. On Maison Panel, open Deck > Configuration > Connecter un PC.
   Sign into the existing Maison owner account in Settings if requested.
4. Enter that eight-digit code into Lantern Deck on the PC and click
   Connect to Maison. Codes expire after five minutes.
5. Check Allow Maison to control scenes and audio on the PC.
6. In the panel's Deck > Configuration, choose your actual Starting,
   Gaming, BRB and Ending scenes from the dropdowns. Save once.

The microphone and desktop-sound buttons use LIVE Studio's audio controls
automatically. Test them offline: tap once, check the mute indicator in
LIVE Studio, then tap again to restore. Test all four scene selections.
Ending changes the scene; it never stops the broadcast.

TEST PC TODAY, WIFE'S PC LATER:
You may leave all scene dropdowns empty while testing audio on Kevin's PC.
Pair your wife's PC later using a new code. That revokes Kevin's connection
and clears the scene selections so you can choose hers. Her login, scenes,
media and audio devices stay on her PC; this program does not copy them.

EVERYDAY USE:
Keep LIVE Studio and Lantern Deck open. Click the Deck tab on Maison.
After each Lantern Deck launch, check Allow Maison to control scenes and
 audio again. The pairing is remembered, but permission starts disabled.
Disconnected or stale controls are disabled; no commands are queued offline.

IF THE PC DOES NOT CONNECT:
Check Internet access, open LIVE Studio's main screen, and read the status
in Lantern Deck. Your version must expose its local controller channel.
The adapter is independent and version-sensitive, not an official TikTok
plugin. If TikTok rejects a command, the panel reports it instead of sending
a keyboard shortcut or pretending it worked.

MOVING / UPDATING:
Copy this ZIP to the other PC and pair it there. Do not copy
%LOCALAPPDATA%\LanternDeck\maison-connection.json: it contains a private key.
Use Deck > Configuration > Deconnecter le PC to revoke a lost PC.
The optional phone shortcut mode remains under its own button in the app.

COST AND CONNECTION:
The custom software has no license fee or subscription. The hosted Maison
relay uses your existing Cloudflare account and Internet connection; free-tier
limits apply. This is not an offline-only connection. No port forwarding is
required. The relay carries only controller status, selected scene names and
commands; it does not transmit your screen, video, camera or microphone audio.
